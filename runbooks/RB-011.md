# RB-011 — customer incident communication
Process: communicating an incident to customers

Policy:
Customer-facing communication should be coordinated through the incident lead and customer/support communication owner.

Guidance:
1. Confirm the incident scope and customer impact before publishing.
2. Communicate confirmed facts rather than speculation.
3. Provide the known impact, current mitigation status, and next update time when available.
4. Keep updates consistent across approved customer communication channels.
5. Publish a resolution update after service recovery and internal confirmation.
